Xurozit

Assam, Northeast India

Hi, I'm Xurozit — a writer, a dreamer, and someone who is deeply in love with the Northeast.

This site is my journal, my archive, and eventually my music room. I write about Assam because it deserves to be written about. The culture, the rivers, the festivals, the food — there's so much here that the world hasn't seen yet.

I also write about the small things. The things I love. The moments that catch you off guard and remind you that life is worth paying attention to.

Music is the next chapter. When I'm ready, it'll live here too — free for anyone to hear.
